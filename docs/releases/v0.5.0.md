# MarginCarry v0.5.0

MarginCarry finds highlights and underlines whose text changed when a word was split or rejoined during PDF layout. It checks line breaks within a page and across two adjacent pages, in either revision.

The dialog shows the original hyphen, including soft hyphens, and both excerpts with their page locations. Choose the location and confirm it manually. Bulk selection skips these candidates. The target rectangles follow the original PDF glyphs and are checked by reading the text back from those rectangles.

## Installation

Download `margincarry-0.5.0.xpi` below. In Zotero, open **Tools → Plugins**, choose **Install Plugin From File…** from the gear menu, and select the XPI. This release supports Zotero 10.0.5 through 10.0.x.

## Verification

In an isolated Zotero 10.0.9 profile on macOS, a mouse-created highlight in LoRA v1 transferred to two lines in LoRA v2 after manual confirmation. Reopening the target showed the highlight on the expected glyphs, and Undo removed the copy. A controlled PDF pair also exercised a word split across pages and rejection of a detached footer. See [Validation](https://github.com/0then0/margincarry/blob/v0.5.0/docs/validation.md).
