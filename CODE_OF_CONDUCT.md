# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in MarginCarry issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces is expected to treat others respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Discuss ideas and code on their merits. Give specific, constructive feedback, accept corrections, and respect boundaries. Use inclusive language and keep disagreement focused on the technical question. Protect other people's private information and research material.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual attention, personal attacks, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Project maintainers moderate project spaces and may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation. Depending on severity and repetition, a response may range from a request to change behavior to a temporary or permanent ban. Maintainers are subject to the same standards and should step aside from handling a report in which they are directly involved.

For an incident visible in an issue or pull request, contact the maintainers in that thread or open a moderation issue with a link. Do not repost abusive content or private details. If a public report would expose someone or the incident involves a maintainer, use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature on the relevant account or content to report privately to GitHub. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Reports should contain only the information necessary to understand the incident. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share personal information only when necessary to investigate or meet legal obligations. This policy does not promise confidentiality for information posted publicly.
